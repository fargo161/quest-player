# Mission Control desk guide

Open `/admin`. Sign in using the shared staff passphrase and your own name or initials. Your operator label is recorded on actions; it is self-reported attribution, not verified individual staff authentication. Sign out when another operator takes over.

1. Search by nickname or Player ID. Nicknames may be duplicated. Ask the player to show the Player ID on their profile, and open the matching account.
2. Confirm the quest has been physically completed according to the event team's separate instructions. Mark it completed. Completion and glass eligibility are recorded together with the audit history.
3. When the person is chosen for a piece, record selection. `ELIGIBLE` alone does not mean selected or handed over.
4. At physical handoff, compare **nickname + Player ID + SELECTED state** with the player. Check the confirmation box only as the piece is physically handed over, then record the handoff. The status becomes `FULFILLED`.

This check prevents choosing the wrong account. It does not establish one account per person.

## Corrections

Use the correction control on the affected quest or reward. Enter a meaningful reason. The original operation remains in the audit history alongside the correction, operator and time.

- Correcting completion returns the quest to ACTIVE and pauses unfulfilled reward eligibility. A new confirmed completion can restore eligibility; make a fresh selection if appropriate.
- Correcting selection returns an unfulfilled reward to ELIGIBLE.
- A fulfilled reward stays FULFILLED. Record an exception explaining what happened; the software cannot reverse a physical handoff.

## Connectivity and recovery

The system requires an online connection. Retry interrupted submissions safely, then inspect the current state before taking another physical action. Never hand over another piece just because the response failed. Refresh the player's record to establish whether the first handoff was recorded.

Players can add and verify an email from their profile. Recovery works only after verification. A Player ID is a public account reference and must never be accepted as a password or recovery credential. Without a verified email, losing the signed-in browser may mean losing access.

Video is optional: players can read the introduction and continue even if media fails. Creative quest instructions and final event assets are separate from this functional interface.
